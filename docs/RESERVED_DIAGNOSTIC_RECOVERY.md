# One-response diagnostic over an unused reservation

This is a private operator workflow, not the ordinary article-generation UI. It
does not grant authority by itself, renew an expired window or reserve money.

## Eligibility and evidence

The typed `expired-reserved-diagnostic-resume` handoff (version 9) can follow one
expired diagnostic whose admission succeeded but whose dispatch never began.
It cannot follow another reserved diagnostic, a pending operation, an uncertain
submission, a non-expiry stop or an already claimed request. Its new purpose is
`reserved-provider-evidence`. A distinct exact approval and separate protected
response consent must be pinned. All historical refresh/admission counters carry
forward under their existing limits.

Freeze the original compact native policy outside both installed and candidate
replacement roots. Preserve its exact bytes and SHA-256, original source approval,
accepted-media evidence, reservation identity, native row/financial hashes and
independent bridge ledger/queue projections. The predecessor journal's successful
admission must match that historical policy and approval. Never replace evidence
by editing a live policy's date or by asserting that a request was not sent.

The existing `ChirpReservationResumePolicy` permits one existing dispatch, zero
new reservations and zero additional reserved funds. Its native resolver still
checks account, project, credential, payload, voice, source revision, pricing,
catalog, financial state, current trust and mapping identity. Only its explicitly
reviewed catalog-derived request revision can change. This workflow permits one
resume admission, then the existing two pre-I/O observations of one correlation
and local attempt. A third observation or different attempt fails closed.

The bridge reads SQLite with the appropriate closed-profile or live WAL-aware
mode. It validates every production-owned claim field, including the queue's
`OutcomeObservedAtUnixMilliseconds`, and compares all other row contents against
the reviewed clean evidence. A claimed request requires the exact installed
mapping, its matching dispatch mapping, consistent local attempt and ordered
mapping/claim times before expiry. A later outcome is never normalized back to
an unused reservation.

## Prepare before choosing the ten-minute window

1. Finish the local source review and synthetic checks. Freeze the helper source,
   native policy snapshot and rollback inputs. Reuse unchanged verified app
   binaries; avoid a rebuild or broad test suite in the timed operation.
2. Reconcile the exact unused reservation and accepted audio. Finish private
   review export, destination selection and all other file pickers before final
   readiness. Prepare the resume-policy template from the native read-only draft;
   do not manufacture its runtime, provider or financial hashes.
3. Establish current clock, profile, package and catalog evidence. If a required
   nonbillable catalog/probe operation needs authority, disclose and bound it
   separately rather than silently using an expired diagnostic window. Measure
   the supported installer path before the timed operation.
4. Obtain exact fresh approval for the same held cent, one unchanged request and
   the existing private-capture protections. Immediately before starting, confirm
   exclusive foreground and enough uninterrupted execution context. Do not begin
   the clock while another task owns the desktop or while awaiting review.
5. Only at actual readiness choose the fixed UTC interval, seal the packet and
   perform the supported transition/admission. This does not reset counters or
   spending. The existing request must be revalidated, never reserved again.
6. Open the already-planned exact-request capture controls, arm one protected
   response, and use the single diagnostic Generate action. Check expiry at
   actual I/O. If any step stalls, fails or expires, stop and reconcile; do not
   silently renew, increase the interval or switch execution routes.
7. Stop after one outcome. Turn capture off and verify the receipt, retained
   commitments, uncertain-submission state and capture-retention metadata.
   No batch continuation is authorized by this diagnostic workflow.

## Verification and limits

- `test_reserved_diagnostic_resume.py`: isolated journal lifecycle, inherited
  counters, restart, one admission, expiry, interrupted activation and replay.
- `test_reserved_diagnostic_rows.py`: production-shaped row/timestamp guards and
  rejection of changed, uncertain, foreign or duplicate evidence.
- `test_reserved_diagnostic_bridge.py`: actual `-I -S` command entry points and
  PowerShell handoff reader against disposable native-schema data. Native mapping
  writes are simulated here; this is not a provider or installed-UI test.
- `Test-ReservedDiagnosticPolicy-SelfTests.ps1`: real native validators for an
  expired historical diagnostic and zero-new-funds resume, including rejections.
- `ExpiredDiagnosticReservationResumesSameCentThroughNativeMappingClaimAndSingleFakeOutcome`:
  existing .NET production services with synthetic provider/account data.

Synthetic acceptance cannot prove that Google will accept the unchanged text or
give a detailed explanation. The actual provider result remains unknown until a
separately authorized request completes. Private response text must stay out of
routine logs, exports, uploads and public source.
